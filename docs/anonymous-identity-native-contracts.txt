Official PC anonymous identity encoding, 3.1.42.205594
====================================================

ABI: core::account::anonymous_identity::encode_anonymous_id(&str) -> Result<String>.
This is a pure local encoder. Its output is neither an anonymous UID nor an
authentication/experiment permission. It does not register, fetch, set Cookies,
adopt an account, or contact the official native host.

The formula, with each URL Base64 retaining padding, is:
  inner = urlBase64(MD5(utf8(deviceId) XOR repeated-key))
  result = urlBase64(utf8(deviceId) || ASCII-space || ASCII(inner))
No trimming, case folding, hexadecimal digest conversion or normalization occurs.
The 4096 UTF-8-byte bound is our explicit local allocation policy, not a recovered
native clamp. Valid UTF-8, including whitespace and NUL, is preserved. Rust &str
does not represent isolated UTF-16 surrogates; malformed CEF conversion behavior
has not been emulated or claimed.
