Sealed search probe request accounting

The ordinary V3, HotExplain card, and carousel WebView probes now obtain the PC
assignment through a genuine MusicHttpClient backed by a closed synthetic HTTP
server. That backend verifies the actual EAPI body, official PC context and the
same local credential used for synthetic account restoration. The full ordered
assignment schema comes from pc_experiments_3_1_42.json, including its duplicates.

The signed backend and the legacy business fixture have separate counters. The
former owns exactly account/get followed by one full assignment; account/get is
not a search read. The latter no longer receives the old three-name assignment.
Do not count a missing old short assignment as a missing production request.

scripts/ci/search_signed_trace.py is shared by these probes and the independent
four-cohort footer proof. It requires the exact signed two-call trace, account
restoration and credential/context checks, the exact full manifest arguments,
and a successful assignment response for ordinary probes. It rejects missing or
duplicate assignments, short or reordered schemas, and extra business reads.

Exact comprehensive search budgets (excluding account restoration):
- --v3: one signed full assignment + one tabs read + one V3 body = 3.
- --hot-card: one signed full assignment + three tabs/V3 pairs = 7.
- --carousel: one signed full assignment + one V1 body = 2. The one body trace
  must retain its exact keyword/scene/channel/cursor/bizQueryInfo arguments.
Back navigation must not add a body read. Existing renderer, playback, queue,
focus, bounded layout and screenshot assertions are preserved.

These proofs use synthetic accounts and isolated/null native audio. They do not
prove real service access, physical sound-card behavior or whole DSP material.
